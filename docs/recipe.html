Tea

Let's steep in happiness sprinkled with tea puns.

keyboard_arrow_down

Ingredients

  • 1 cup filtered water
  • 1 tsp black gold tea
  • 1/2 cup fresh milk
  • a pinch of masala
  • sugar (optional)

Preparation Steps

  1. 01 Boil water in a small pot with the masala.
  2. 02 Add the tea leaves and steep on low heat for 3 minutes.
  3. 03 Pour in the milk and bring to a rolling boil.
  4. 04 Strain into a ceramic cup and sweeten if desired.
  5. Note (You can make this as per your taste also.)

Green Tea (Kahwa)

Natural active immunity booster.

keyboard_arrow_down

Ingredients

  • 1 cup filtered water
  • 1 tsp green gold tea(kahwa) leaves
  • a pinch of masala(optional as per your taste)
  • sugar (optional)
  • cardamom (in case you want to add it)

Preparation Steps

  1. 01 Boil water in a small pot with cardamom(if you want to add it).
  2. 02 Once the water boils properly turn off he stove.
  3. 03 Then after 2 seconds add on kahwa leaves and leave it for 2 minutes .
  4. 04 Then strain it into cup and you can add few drops of lemon or sugar also as per your taste.
